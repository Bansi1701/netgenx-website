Cloud Solutions

Move to the cloud without losing control of security or spend.

From stalled lift-and-shift projects to sprawling multi-cloud estates, NetGenX designs, secures, and runs your cloud across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, with a landing zone, guardrails, and FinOps discipline built in from day one.

The challenge

The cloud promised speed. For most enterprises, it delivered drift.

Migrations stall, ownership blurs, and the bill keeps climbing. We see the same four failure modes in nearly every estate we assess.

Stalled migrations

Lift-and-shift projects that miss their date by quarters, blocked by undocumented dependencies, legacy auth, and a fear of breaking production.

Unclear security ownership

The shared-responsibility model gets misread. Cloud teams assume the provider has it covered; security assumes the cloud team does. Gaps live in the seams.

Misconfigurations

Public S3 buckets, over-permissive IAM roles, and unencrypted volumes. The vast majority of cloud breaches trace back to a setting nobody reviewed.

Runaway cloud spend

Idle instances, orphaned storage, and zero showback. Finance can't tie cost to a team or product, so budgets balloon with no accountability.

Compliance blind spots

Auditors want evidence on demand. Without continuous posture management, every SOC 2 cycle turns into a manual fire drill across consoles.

Multi-cloud sprawl

Three providers, four tooling stacks, and no single source of truth. Inconsistent guardrails make every new workload a fresh risk decision.

How we do it

A migration playbook that secures as it scales.

Four disciplined phases that take you from a foggy estate to a governed, cost-aware cloud you can actually defend.

Assess & map dependencies

We discover every workload, data flow, and integration, then score each for migration readiness, security exposure, and business criticality. You leave with a prioritized wave plan, not a wish list.

Build a secure landing zone

Using infrastructure as code, we stand up a multi-account landing zone with network segmentation, encryption defaults, centralized logging, and identity-based access, the guardrails every future workload inherits automatically.

Migrate in governed waves

We re-host, re-platform, or re-architect each workload to fit, validating against the landing zone's policies before cutover. Every wave is scanned for misconfigurations so nothing ships with a hole in it.

Operate, optimize & prove

Continuous posture management, FinOps rightsizing, and audit-ready evidence, delivered as a managed service. We hold the watch so your team ships features instead of chasing alerts.

Productivity platforms

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, chosen on fit, then run properly.

Microsoft and Google build the platforms. NetGenX is the team that picks the right one for your estate, migrates you onto it without losing mail, configures the security baseline, and keeps running it afterward.

Microsoft 365

Microsoft's productivity cloud for organizations standardized on Windows and Outlook, with email, files, meetings and endpoint security in one console.

  • Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams, with desktop Office from Business Standard up
  • Business Basic through Business Premium cap at 300 users; E3, E5 and F3 above that
  • Conditional access and multifactor authentication through Entra ID P1, admin roles delegated by scope
  • Defender for Business, Safe Links and Safe Attachments, plus Intune on Business Premium
  • Purview data loss prevention, sensitivity labels and eDiscovery across Exchange, SharePoint and OneDrive

Google Workspace

Google's browser-first suite for teams that work in Gmail and Docs, administered from a single console with low IT overhead.

  • Gmail on your domain, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Chat, Calendar and Gemini
  • Drive storage from 30 GB to 5 TB per user, and Meet holding 100 to 1,000 participants
  • One Admin console covers users, organizational units and Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and ChromeOS
  • Remote wipe and passcode rules from Business Plus, with Vault retention and legal hold
  • Enterprise editions add data loss prevention, Context-Aware Access and client-side encryption

Moving between them is routine work here. We have run 300+ mailbox migrations, Google to Microsoft, Microsoft to Zoho and Zimbra to Microsoft, carrying devices and user profiles across with the mail.
