End-User Computing

Devices ready on day one, managed for every day after.

We buy, build, ship and support the machines your people work on, from the first-day laptop to the one being wiped on the way out. One standard, one management console, and a spare on the shelf when hardware fails.

Talk to the team See all services
The challenge

The cost of a slow start

Device problems rarely look urgent on their own, which is how they end up costing a week of someone's time.

Day one, no laptop

Hiring tells IT a week late, the model is out of stock, and a new starter spends their first days on a borrowed machine.

Every build is different

Machines imaged by hand drift by model and by technician, so a fault on one laptop never reproduces on the next and fixes stay guesswork.

Patches that never land

Remote laptops miss the maintenance window, users defer the restart for weeks, and the compliance report flatters a fleet that is months behind.

How we do it

One build, repeated reliably.

We own the physical device at every stage: what gets bought, built, fixed and wiped, not just the record of it.

Standards and purchasing

We cut the catalog to a few models per role and extend it to the rest of the floor: printers, UPS units, cameras. Specs, warranty terms and a spares shelf are agreed up front.

Zero-touch enrollment

Devices are registered with the vendor before they ship, so a sealed box goes straight to the user and builds itself on first sign-in. No technician unboxes it, no image per model.

One policy baseline

Windows, Mac and mobile under one baseline: encryption with escrowed keys, app delivery, patch rings tested on a pilot group first. A bad update then stops at a few machines.

Repair, return, retire

Warranty claims and loaner swaps keep someone working while a machine is out. At exit we collect the device, verify the wipe and document disposal or resale.

Tools we use

Platform-neutral, chosen to fit your estate.

We deploy and operate what suits your environment and licensing, rather than reselling one stack to everybody.
